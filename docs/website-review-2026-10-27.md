# Debbie Welch Homes: current-site review

**Review date:** October 27, 2026, UTC. **Nothing published or deployed.**

## Executive assessment

The current website is recognizable, personal and useful. Its strongest content explains decisions rather than promising outcomes. The newer county, Arlington and Snohomish guides, the downsizing guidance, and several revised articles are good examples to retain. This is not a recommendation to redesign the site.

The highest-priority work for tomorrow is narrowly focused: neutralize school-quality and neighborhood-safety implications, substantiate dated figures, qualify financial/contractual absolutes, review the privacy policy with qualified advice, and settle the future-domain launch configuration. Existing personal details and testimonials should not be replaced with generic brokerage language.

### Evidence labels

- **Confirmed:** directly observed in current source, rendered pages, or an identified original source. A confirmed quotation is not confirmation that its factual claim is true.
- **Inferred:** a professional judgment about clarity, voice, risk or visitor flow, not a proven legal or search-ranking violation.
- **Unverified:** available evidence does not establish the claim. This does not automatically mean it is false.

## 6. SEO, accessibility and future indexing

### Confirmed working elements

All 53 rendered public pages had one H1 and one canonical. Thirty-eight rendered JSON-LD blocks parsed as valid JSON; parsing is not full schema/rich-result validation. Titles/descriptions are route-specific. Existing production HTML contains substantive body content and route metadata. The site uses React with build-time SSR/prerendering; request-time SSR was not established. Keep the existing architecture.

The current fonts, approved photographs, eXp logo, shared script styles, warm photo overlays and localized area-title backdrops were retained. Visual differences among page structures are not automatically defects. Long scripts wrap on phones without measured overflow. No font-size redesign was made.

### SEO findings and proposals

1. **Current host versus future canonical domain:** source metadata, robots sitemap directive and sitemap entries target `debbiewelchhomes.com`. That domain currently returned a 301 to eXp; its robots/sitemap returned 404 during inspection. The current foundation scan flags host mismatch for sitemap and robots. These findings remain unresolved, not marked fixed. They are a launch/deployment decision, not a reason to silently change your intended domain.
2. **Sitemap dates:** all 53 entries use a fixed `2026-08-17` fallback. Proposed repair is to omit unsupported lastmod values or maintain truthful page-specific dates, never set every page to today's build date. Generator was not changed.
3. **Conditional prerender defect:** metadata/generator support a site URL override but prerender extraction hardcodes the intended hostname. An override can produce zero prerendered routes. Propose origin-independent extraction and an explicit failure on zero routes, retaining the existing mechanism.
4. **Eighteen article routes are public but absent from blog listing/search.** Add curated topic/related links or decide intentional curation. Do not automatically delete, change routes, or claim a duplicate-content penalty.
5. **Metadata maintenance drift:** some unused area entries in `seoData.ts` differ from page-supplied metadata. Rendered head was not duplicated. Propose one clearly documented source of truth, not a new SEO system.
6. **Invalid URL behavior:** establish genuine HTTP 404 responses and suitable not-found metadata. Client navigation does not prove server status behavior.
7. **Business schema:** consider a stable entity identifier and truthful references. Do not invent a street address, rating, credential, service or review. FAQ markup does not promise Google rich results.
8. **Social previews:** ordinary pages often omit an explicit image. Hosting may provide a fallback. Check actual shared-link results after launch before choosing an existing brand image; no new image was made.

- Shared skip-to-content link and route-change focus management.
